Detail++

Lifeng Chen, Jiner Wang, Zihao Pan, Beier Zhu, Xiaofeng Yang, and Chi Zhang.

Project page · Paper

The animation uses original image panels from an author-supplied version of Figure 2. Moving prompt cards explain decomposition and parallel branches sharing self-attention from the full prompt. An explicit base-latent and two-update chain illustrates early-stage Accumulative Latent Modification, retaining earlier content outside each subject mask. The ending compares the complete-prompt baseline with the final accumulated Detail++ result under the same full prompt. These complete images are published examples; the latent update diagram is schematic, and the presentation does not depict elapsed inference time or live inference.

GENESIS

Fan Yang, Lifeng Chen, Yipu Wang, Han Wang, and coauthors.

The illustrated reasoning trace faithfully paraphrases the object attributes and reasoning accompanying appendix Figure 6 in the manuscript supplied by Lifeng Chen, using natural sentences in place of its parenthetical attribute list. The base scene was edited with AI to remove its baked-in annotations. The six objects and their relevant attributes were checked against Figure 6. Annotation history is scoped to each paragraph: the current primitive has a white contour and colored label border, while previously referenced primitives in that paragraph have neutral gray contours and labels. History clears smoothly at paragraph boundaries. Complete text phrases use a single primitive color, including their connecting words, and earlier phrases become gray. Label geometry remains fixed throughout. The final paragraph identifies four remaining objects. This simulated autoregressive illustration is not live model execution. Rights remain with the original authors.
