GENESIS

Fan Yang, Lifeng Chen, Yipu Wang, Han Wang, and coauthors.

The illustrated reasoning trace follows the chain of thought accompanying appendix Figure 6 in the manuscript supplied by Lifeng Chen: observe the scene, identify each object's visual attributes, evaluate the question's compound conditions against the visible evidence, and derive the remaining set and answer. Presentation highlights connect the attributes and logical predicates to the original scene. Rights remain with the original authors.
