Biography

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Georgia. My research interests lie in multimodal foundation models, with a focus on multimodal reasoning, unified understanding and generation, and agentic reinforcement learning.

Previously, I was a research intern at Xiaohongshu's Super Intelligence Team, Midea Group's AI Research Center, and the VLAA Lab at UC Santa Cruz, and a visiting student at Westlake University. I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science from Beijing Jiaotong University in 2026.
