About me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Georgia, where I am fortunate to be advised by Distinguished Research Professor Tianming Liu. My research interests lie in multimodal foundation models, with a focus on multimodal reasoning, multimodal generation, and world models.

Previously, I was a research intern at Xiaohongshu's Super Intelligence Team and Midea Group's AI Research Center. I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science from Beijing Jiaotong University in 2026, where I have had the privilege of working closely with Xiaojie Jin. I also spent time as a visiting student in the AGI Lab at Westlake University, advised by Chi Zhang.
